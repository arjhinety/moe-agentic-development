## Compute

- **[AMD AI Developer Program](https://developer.amd.com/)** — free AMD GPU
  access. Listed as a main contributor to this project: the benchmark design
  calls for a four-arm, six-task, n≥5 experiment (~120 agent runs, plus the
  training and profiling behind Tasks 2 and 4). The first wave's harness ran on
  an MI300X through the program; the agent under test ran through an API, so
  that wave used no GPU inference or training. The Task 2 and 4 training and
  profiling (Phase 2.5) is planned for that hardware.

  This entry records compute support. The AMD AI Developer Program does not
  author, review, or endorse this work.
